§ 01

Research

Given a huge set of items, which few should you keep so that together they cover as much variety as possible? Real data is noisy, and a single outlier can fool the obvious approaches. With my advisors I designed algorithms that stay robust to outliers and still scale to very large inputs.

With
Andrea Pietracaprina, Geppino Pucci
